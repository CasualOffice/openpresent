Your PowerPoint files, in the browser.

OpenPresent opens a .pptx and shows its slides: a thumbnail strip, the slide, and a full-screen slide show. The file is read in your browser by our own engine compiled to WebAssembly, and never uploaded.

Open a presentation See the slide gallery

What works today

Open any deck

Pick a file or drop it on the window. A file the engine cannot read is named with its error code, never silently dropped.

See every slide

Shapes, fills, lines, pictures and text, drawn by the engine. Arrow keys, clicks and the thumbnail pane move between slides.

Present

Slide Show from the beginning or the current slide, full screen.

Honest about gaps

What a slide uses that is not drawn yet is listed, by feature, in the status bar.

What comes next

Tables, charts and effects are being drawn now. Editing, saving and collaboration follow. The ribbon already shows PowerPoint's commands; the ones not built yet are disabled and say so.
